Standard Deviation Visualizer

Standard deviation is the typical distance of the data values from their mean. Drag the points: squeeze them toward the mean and the standard deviation shrinks; spread them out and it grows. The bars under each point show its deviation — the farther a point sits from the mean, the more it pulls the standard deviation up.

Target challenge: arrange the points so the standard deviation lands within 0.2 of the target.

Notice: one point dragged far away moves the standard deviation much more than several points nudged a little. Squaring the deviations is why — distance counts double, then counts again.
